## Tactical Move Pool

### 1. `spout_splash`
- **Type**: Water
- **Power**: 24
- **Accuracy**: 95%
- **Coin Cost**: 0
- **Effect**: Standard water pressure jet.

### 2. `bubble_barrier`
- **Type**: Water
- **Power**: 0 (Defensive Shield)
- **Accuracy**: 100%
- **Coin Cost**: 0 (Free cooldown move)
- **Effect**: Shield (100% chance, 1 tick duration)
- **Description**: Projects an aqueous shield that nullifies the next attack, keeping allies alive against lethal strikes.

### 3. `tidal_surge`
- **Type**: Water
- **Power**: 42
- **Accuracy**: 85%
- **Coin Cost**: 2 coins
- **Effect**: High-tide lane sweep.
- **Description**: Spends 2 coins to unleash a rushing water torrent, washing over fire and stone foes.

## Synergies & Team Compositions

- **Optimal Partner**: `ember_fox` (Fire) or `volt_hedgehog` (Electric).
  - Shields fragile damage dealers so they can safely channel expensive tactical abilities.
- **Counters**: Fire bruisers (`ember_fox`, `flame_salamander`) and Stone tanks (`crag_golem`).
- **Vulnerabilities**: Electric rapid-fire zaps (`volt_hedgehog`, `spark_finch`).
