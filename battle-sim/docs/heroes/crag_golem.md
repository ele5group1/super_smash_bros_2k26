## Synergies & Team Compositions

- **Optimal Partner**: `moss_shroom` (Grass Healer) or `spark_finch` (Electric Scout).
  - Finch secures initial initiative, Moss Shroom heals off attrition, Golem finishes late-game.
- **Counters**: Fire strikers (`ember_fox`, `flame_salamander`) and Electric blitzers (`volt_hedgehog`, `spark_finch`).
- **Weakness**: Grass botanical drains (`moss_shroom`) and heavy Water rushes (`bubble_toad`).
