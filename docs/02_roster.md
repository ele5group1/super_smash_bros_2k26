## Draft rules

- Each faction offers 5 characters. A legal team has exactly 3 distinct characters with total cost at most 12.
- Cost 5 (Tank) plus two cost-4 characters totals 13 and is illegal. Cost 5 plus any two cost-3 characters totals 11 and is legal.
- There are 60 ordered trios per faction (5P3). Legal trios exclude the ones over budget.
- Lineup order is chosen after the draft and locked before battle.

## Balance notes

- Mirror roles are symmetric. Faction parity must hold under identical policies with swapped labels.
- Any role with a win rate more than 5 points above the others, without explanation, is a balance bug.
- Specials are symmetric in value by design, so differences in win rate should come from roster and draft choices, not from special power.
